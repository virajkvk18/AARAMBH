# AARAMBH - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Overview
AARAMBH is an integrated Single Window Clearance Portal designed to streamline, automate, and accelerate industrial approvals, licenses, incentives, and clearances.

## 2. Core Pillars
- **Intelligent KYA (Know Your Approvals)**: Dynamic questionnaire-based engine determining all required department clearances.
- **Unified Common Application Form (CAF)**: Single form capturing applicant, enterprise, land, and operational details with auto-population.
- **AI Document Vault & Extraction**: Automated OCR and schema-based document validation (via DigiLocker, PyMuPDF, EasyOCR, Groq LLMs).
- **Automated Workflows & Parallel Approvals**: Clear multi-department routing with SLA tracking and deemed approval workflows.
- **Grievance Redressal & Incentive Management**: Transparent dispute tracking, query management, and subsidy disbursement.
